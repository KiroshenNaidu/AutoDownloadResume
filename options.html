You probably don't need to change these.

Firefox sometimes reports a dropped connection as "cancelled by you". When the extension can't tell which it was, ticked means it leaves the download alone (safe). Unticked means it retries it — but it will then also retry downloads you really did cancel.

seconds

A download that is "cancelled" this soon after your connection dropped is treated as a network failure and retried.

Downloads you pause yourself while online are always left alone.

minutes

Firefox puts the extension to sleep when idle; this check is what wakes it. Retries waiting longer than about 25 seconds happen on this check, so a bigger number here can make them later.
